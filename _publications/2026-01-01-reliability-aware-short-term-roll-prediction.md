## Abstract

This work investigates short-term roll prediction for unmanned surface vehicles (USVs) under conditions where prediction reliability is important. The proposed approach combines multi-task learning with adaptive centralization to improve the robustness and reliability of short-horizon roll prediction.

The manuscript is currently **under review** and is listed as an **SCI Q2 TOP** paper in the curriculum vitae.
